CyberSafe campaign

“Think Before You Click. Protect Before You Share.”

Purpose

The CyberSafe campaign exists to convert complex cybersecurity habits into practical everyday actions. We aim to help families, students, employees, and community members recognize risk early and make safer choices online.

  • Build confidence in recognizing phishing tricks.
  • Reduce personal-data leakage through social media habits.
  • Create better password routines and stronger account security.
  • Support safer payment and online transaction decisions.

Campaign methods

We combine education, reflection, scenario-based tasks, and simple tools that encourage quick action.

How to run this campaign in a community setting

1. Start with awareness

Present real scenarios involving weak passwords, shared personal data, and suspicious messages.

2. Use the interactive tools

Let participants test password strength and complete the data-protection checklist.

3. Compare before and after

Use the pre/post awareness assessment so the group can see learning progress.

4. Share resources

Print or share the summaries and guidelines to keep the message alive after the workshop.
