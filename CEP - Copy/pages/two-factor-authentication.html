Password Hygiene

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a second step after your password, such as an approval prompt or one-time code. Even if a password is stolen, the attacker still needs the second factor.

For many users, 2FA is the easiest and strongest improvement in account security. It protects email, banking, social media, and other sensitive accounts, especially when they are frequently targeted by phishing or credential theft.

Use 2FA on

  • Email accounts
  • Banking and financial apps
  • Social media and cloud storage
  • Work or study platforms

Be careful

  • Do not share one-time codes with anyone.
  • Do not approve random prompts you did not initiate.
  • Use an authenticator app or trusted security key where available.

Key takeaways

  • 2FA adds a stronger barrier even when passwords are exposed.
  • App-based approval and security keys are generally safer than SMS-only options.
  • Set it up on your most valuable accounts first.
