Campaign updates

Recent campaigns we have conducted

“Think Before You Click. Protect Before You Share.”

Our recent campaigns have focused on creating awareness in communities by helping people understand everyday digital risks, safe online habits, and practical ways to protect personal information. Through on-ground sessions, interactive discussions, and awareness activities, we have reached students, families, and local groups with simple guidance on password safety, phishing awareness, privacy protection, and secure online behavior.

How to run this campaign in a community setting

1. Start with awareness

Present real scenarios involving weak passwords, shared personal data, and suspicious messages.

2. Use the interactive tools

Let participants test password strength and complete the data-protection checklist.

3. Compare before and after

Use the pre/post awareness assessment so the group can see learning progress.

4. Share resources

Print or share the summaries and guidelines to keep the message alive after the workshop.
